# 🌿 BudDeals
Windows app that finds the best dispensary deals near any ZIP/city/GPS (5–150 mi), ranks them by category, plans your budget, and reads your rewards points after you sign in.

**Install:** download `BudDeals-Setup.exe` from [Releases](../../releases/latest) and run it. The app checks for updates every time it opens.

- Dispensaries come from OpenStreetMap. Deals are read live from each dispensary's public website (re-read after 3 h, or hit 🔄 Refresh).
- Rewards: Wallet → Open & sign in → go to your rewards page → Read my rewards. Logins and rewards stay on your PC (`%APPDATA%\BudDeals`).
- Always confirm deals in store. For adults 21+ / registered patients where legal.
